Nine One Technology

Professional sport has shown what technology can reveal.

Everyday sport does not need to become professional sport. It can use technology in its own way: to make a session more enjoyable, make a game more memorable and give people more reasons to play together again.

Make progress visible.

Give players a clearer view of the improvement they can feel from one session to the next.

Make every game more fun.

Use technology to add feedback, moments and a little more enjoyment to the game itself.

Keep people connected.

Make the moments worth sharing easier to find, remember and bring back to the group.

Smart gear

Equipment that adds to the game.

Smart rackets can make the effort in every session easier to understand. The aim is not more numbers. It is a better feeling of progress when you step back onto the court.

AI court

A court that can hold onto the moment.

Integrated cameras and sensing can capture highlights and effort as the game unfolds. The technology stays quiet. The experience comes back to the player when it is useful.

The foundation for a better experience.

Booking is only the start. Discovery, access and club insight make the day easier, so the sport itself has more room to be enjoyable.
