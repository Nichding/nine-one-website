Built to stay safe and open.

Every structure carries an operations layer that watches the things that matter outdoors.

01

Wind-load monitoring

Sensors track the load on the structure in real time, so the team sees pressure building before it becomes a problem.

02

Wind-speed estimation

On-site readings model conditions across the site, giving an accurate picture without a weather station on every corner.

03

Structural safety

Thresholds and alerts are set to the structure's limits, so operators know when to act and when conditions are clear.
