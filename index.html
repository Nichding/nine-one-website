The problem

The sporting gene is here.The facilities have not kept up.

Outdoor by default

Most Kiwi courts and fields sit in the open — and the weather keeps its own schedule.

Imported by habit

Domes and courts arrive off the shelf from overseas — designed for other sites, other seasons.

Paid for by players

Built at a premium and half-empty, venues pass the cost down — and the price of playing climbs.

Nine One exists to change all three.
