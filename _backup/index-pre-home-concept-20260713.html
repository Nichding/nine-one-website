The problem

Communities are short of play, not demand.

Demand for court time keeps growing. The hours to meet it keep shrinking — for three reasons that have nothing to do with sport.

01

Weather

Rain, wind and winter evenings take bookable hours off courts a community already owns.

02

Land

New land for sport is scarce and slow to secure. The space that exists has to work harder.

03

Buildings

A traditional indoor hall is expensive and slow — so most projects stop at the estimate.

None of this is a demand problem. It's a design problem — and that's where we start.
