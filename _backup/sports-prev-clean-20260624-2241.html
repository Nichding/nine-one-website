The system

A new kind of play infrastructure.

Nine One Play isn't a court you buy. It's a complete system — surface, structure, lighting, access and operations — that turns a piece of land into a sport space a community can use, run and come back to. Designed to stand up to weather, fill with people, and keep earning its place long after opening day.

All year

Rain, wind, winter, dark evenings — built for play through more of the year.

Covered, protected and bookable — designed to keep people playing through the seasons that usually empty a court.

05 — Quiet technology

The smart parts of a Nine One venue work in the background — keeping the space safe, open and easy to run, without ever getting in the way of play.

Wind-load monitoring

Covered structures can be monitored against conditions, helping keep the space safe in weather.

Structural safety
