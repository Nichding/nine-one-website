# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Community-sport decision makers, players and prospective venue partners exploring Nine One's play, technology and facility offering. A separate Shop audience visits to discover and buy considered racket-sport equipment.

## Product Purpose

Nine One presents a connected, year-round sport ecosystem, helping visitors understand the relationship between places to play, the technology around play and the facilities that make them possible. The Shop is an independent retail destination for equipment and essentials.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, precise and active. The main site should feel like a confident sports-infrastructure partner. The Shop should feel like a focused specialist retailer, direct and useful rather than promotional.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic ecommerce clutter, discount-led visual noise, oversized promotional badges and lifestyle-sport clichés. Avoid decorative UI that obscures the product or the path to action.

## Design Principles

- Let real sport, venues and equipment do the visual work.
- Keep navigation and choices immediately legible.
- Make the relationship between play, technology and facilities concrete.
- Use restraint as a sign of confidence, not as a substitute for hierarchy.
- Preserve a clear, accessible path from discovery to action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access. Respect reduced-motion preferences and support responsive reading and shopping across mobile and desktop.
