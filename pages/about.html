About Us

Move
Together.

Sport was always meant to bring people together — where movement meets community, and space meets intelligence.

Why We Exist

The world is more connected than ever — yet people have never felt more physically apart. Sports infrastructure hasn't kept pace. Most venues are fragmented, transactional, and built without community in mind. We believe movement should feel different. It should be social. Intelligent. Deeply human. Nine ONE exists to build a new generation of movement infrastructure — spaces and systems that bring people together, not just bring them in.

Our Philosophy

Calm Movement.

We don't believe sport is about winning. We believe it is about belonging.

Movement should reduce stress, not create it. It should bring people together, not isolate them. Technology should enhance the experience, not overwhelm it. Every court we build, every system we design, every community we support — exists around this one belief.

Move together. Build together. Grow together.

Our Origin

Designed in New Zealand. Built for the world.

New Zealand is where Nine ONE began. Not by accident — but by design. Small enough to move fast. Trusted enough to build real relationships. Open enough to think differently about what sport and community can be. This is where we proved the model. This is where the idea became real.

Our Direction

Our journey has just begun.

Now

Building our foundation in New Zealand. Creating iconic venues and connected experiences.

Next

Expanding across Australia and the region. Growing our community and technology ecosystem.

Future

Exporting Nine ONE infrastructure into Asia and the world. Setting a new standard for movement everywhere.
